## Form is a verb

Atelier Zirkel is a cooperative of architects, designers and artists who share
one conviction and one colour chart. We build reading pavilions and bathhouses,
revive forgotten alphabets, hang balance studies in turbine halls, and repaint
grey underpasses in primaries. The work spans three disciplines and a dozen
countries; the attitude does not change at the border.

A century ago a school in Weimar taught that art and industry belong at the
same workbench. We take that as a standing instruction.
