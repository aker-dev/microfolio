## The short version

This site sets **no cookies**, runs **no analytics**, and asks you to accept
nothing. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

## What is stored on your device

One value, and only if you ask for it: when you use the light/dark switch in the
footer, your choice is kept in your browser's local storage under the key
`theme`. It holds `light` or `dark`, nothing else. It never leaves your browser,
and no one else can read it.

Under French rules this kind of interface preference is exempt from consent,
because it does only what you asked it to do. Clearing your browser data removes
it.

## What reaches other companies

Loading any web page means your device asks a server for files, and a server
cannot answer without knowing your IP address. Three are involved here:

| Who          | What for                   | When                 |
| ------------ | -------------------------- | -------------------- |
| GitHub, Inc. | hosting this site          | every page           |
| Bunny.net    | the IBM Plex Sans typeface | every page           |
| OpenFreeMap  | the map tiles              | only on the map page |

We do not control what those companies do with the requests they receive. Their
own policies apply: [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/site-policy),
[Bunny.net](https://bunny.net/privacy), [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org).

## What we do not do

No analytics or audience measurement. No advertising or tracking scripts. No
social media embeds. No contact form. No account, no login. We collect nothing
about you and hold no database.

## Your rights

If you contact us by email we will have your message and your address, and
nothing more. Under the GDPR you may ask for access, correction or deletion, and
you may complain to the CNIL.

Write to [hello@aker.pro](mailto:hello@aker.pro).
